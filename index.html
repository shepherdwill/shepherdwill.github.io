Will Shepherd

Research Assistant, London School of Economics

I work in the Growth Programme at the Centre for Economic Performance and the Growth Lab at the School of Public Policy, with Professor Richard Davies. My research looks at business dynamism in the UK: how firms enter, grow and exit, and how workers move between firms and places.

I am interested in innovative uses of data, especially large-scale administrative data, to understand economic and societal trends, and in informing public policy through simple, clear communication.
